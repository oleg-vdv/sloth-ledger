# sloth-ledger

Since January 2026, Kazakh banks report a personal card to the tax office
when it receives transfers from **100 or more different people in each of
three consecutive months**, totalling **more than 12 minimum wages**
(1 020 000 ₸ in 2026). In the first quarter alone that was about 114 000
people: home bakers, tutors, people who split rent with flatmates, people
whose relatives send money. Each got a notice asking them to explain where the
money came from.

Explaining it means going through hundreds of transfers by hand and sorting
them into "customer", "mum", "flatmate paying her half of the rent". Nobody
wants to do that. This does it for you, on your own machine.

You give it:

- the card statement (Kaspi Gold PDF, plain text or CSV), and
- two or three sentences in your own words: what you sell, who your relatives
  are, who you share the flat with.

You get:

- the 100/3/12 check, month by month, computed from the statement — not by a
  model;
- every sender sorted into customer / family / shared expenses / loan /
  unclear, with a one-line reason;
- a short list of the senders the model was **not** sure about — the only ones
  you need to look at;
- a draft reply to the notice in which every number and every name comes from
  the statement, never from the model.

## Why an open model, locally

A card statement is the most personal document most people have: names,
amounts, the shape of their week. Sending it to a hosted API to answer a
letter from the tax office would be its own small privacy incident. The
classifier is an open-weight model (Gemma 4 by default) served by
[Ollama](https://ollama.com) on your computer or on a box in your home
network. Nothing in this project calls the internet.

## What the model does and doesn't do

| Part | Done by |
|---|---|
| Reading the statement | regex over the PDF text |
| The 100/3/12 rule | code (`sloth_ledger/rule.py`) |
| Grouping transfers by person | code |
| Deciding who each person is | **the open model** |
| Confidence and "ask the human" | the model, plus guard rails |
| The letter | a template filled by code |

Guard rails around the model: it sees one compact profile per sender, never
the raw ledger; the code, not the model, decides which senders the notes talk
about; senders it invents are dropped; senders it skips, malformed JSON and
unknown categories become "unclear"; and calling someone family, a flatmate
or a lender — "this is not income" — without the owner's note is treated as a
guess and goes to the human.

## Run

```bash
ollama pull gemma4:12b
python -m sloth_ledger --model gemma4:12b --ollama http://127.0.0.1:11434
```

Open http://localhost:8765, press «Взять пример» to load a synthetic
statement, then «Разобрать выписку». Python 3.11+, standard library only;
`pypdf` is needed for PDF statements.

## Measured

`python bench.py <model> ...` runs the judge against a synthetic statement
with known answers (`samples/aigerim`, 205 senders, generated by
`sloth_ledger/synth.py`) and writes `docs/bench.json` and
[docs/bench.md](docs/bench.md). On an RTX 5050 laptop:

| Model | Time | Sent to you to check | Wrong and confident |
|---|---|---|---|
| `gemma4:12b` | 17 min 23 s | 9 of 205 | 0 |
| `llama3.1:8b` | 2 min 50 s | 69 of 205 | 0 |
| `gemma4:e2b-it-qat` | 1 min 42 s | 200 of 205 | 0 |

Zero confident mistakes is the guard rails' doing; the model decides how
much work is left for you.

## Limits

- Senders are identified by the name the bank prints ("Айгерим Б."). Two
  different people with the same first name and initial merge into one.
- The letter explains what the transfers were. Whether and how to declare
  income is your decision; this is a draft, not tax advice.
- The sample statement is synthetic. No real statement was used to build or
  test this.
